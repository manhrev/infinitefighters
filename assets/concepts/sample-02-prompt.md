## Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: landscape pixel-art game asset approval board v02 for TASK VO TAN, a corporate satire 2D side-view platformer.
Primary request: much wider, roomier MULTIPLAYER arena than a cramped action screenshot. The main approval criterion is usable empty movement space for four players. Create one cohesive 1536x1024 landscape image.
Style: crisp polished 16-bit pixel art, consistent pixel grid, navy and cyan office, cream platform tops, coral red danger. Charming fictional office workers, no real person likeness. Flat orthographic SIDE VIEW, not isometric.
Composition: small title strip at top reading "TASK VO TAN" and "MULTIPLAYER ARENA / SAMPLE 02". The upper 76 percent of the image is ONE large panoramic gameplay view. The bottom 20 percent is a compact neatly separated asset reference strip. Avoid giant character portraits or decorative borders that waste space.
MAIN GAMEPLAY VIEW: a cavernous open-plan office floor at night, overlooking skyline through tall glass windows. Show a complete spacious arena with a long continuous floor and six floating desk-like platform ledges across three staggered vertical levels. Platforms are broad and separated by clearly jumpable gaps, with generous clear air and landing room. Horizontal tops obvious, slim supports do not obstruct traversal. Leave broad open ground corridors and a large free zone both to the left and right of the boss. Faint background office furniture is decorative, contrasting less than platforms.
EXTREMELY IMPORTANT SCALE: playable heroes are each about 5 percent of the gameplay panel HEIGHT, not large illustrations. Boss is about 12-14 percent of panel height, only 2.5 times hero height. World occupies most pixels, with abundant negative space, not a tight crop. Four players evenly spread across arena: P1 teal hoodie developer on left ground wielding keyboard; P2 violet on a left upper platform; P3 amber visibly jumping between middle platforms; P4 mint on a right middle platform. Small readable P1 P2 P3 P4 labels above them. Exactly four players. One stopwatch PM boss at right-center ground in suit holding big stopwatch, visually funny and angry, with open floor continuing beyond him to right checkpoint elevator. Only a few red task-card projectiles and one understated floor attack warning, never fill the whole arena with particles. Characters face appropriate directions. Running, jumping, platforms and melee must read unmistakably as a 2D platformer. Thin DEADLINE healthbar at upper center, tiny four colored player status chips upper left.
BOTTOM ASSET STRIP: four small same-sized teal dev sprites idle/run/jump/keyboard swing; one stopwatch PM boss sprite at 2.5 times hero size; small separated floor tile, desk ledge tile, server prop, coffee health pickup, document collectible, key and red task-card projectile. Label groups only "PLAYER", "BOSS", "TILES", "ITEMS". Do not place full scenic backgrounds behind these small assets, keep plain dark navy board background.
Constraints: match coherent pixel-art visual language across gameplay and assets. Side view only. No top-down, no 3D rendering, no photographic textures, no huge characters, no crowded arena, no checkerboard background, no watermark. All bodies fully visible. This is an approval concept board, not a runtime sprite atlas.
